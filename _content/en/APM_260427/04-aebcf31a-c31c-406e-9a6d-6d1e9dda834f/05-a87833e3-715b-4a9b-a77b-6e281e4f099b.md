**Post-process: The finishing touch of direction that goes beyond simple generation to breathe life into the work.**

It is the final stage of completing the direction, going beyond simple image coloring work. 

Let us introduce the post-process areas available through the AlphaLab solution: Creating the overall atmosphere by applying light (light sources), shadows, and colors; adjusting the overall tone according to the weather, time of day (day/night/sunset), and emotional lines; and enhancing immersion by adjusting brightness or darkness. 

Adding supernatural effects such as magic effects, flames, dust, and blowing wind. It is a feature that seamlessly composites images of specific spaces and character images.

- Post-process cases 

  Method 1
- Attach the image to be changed.  
- Enter the command (prompt).  
- Click the Generate button.

  **ㄴ Example keywords: change time of day, change lighting color, add fog effect, add spark effect**

  Method 2: Utilizing a reference image

  Select Edit+Fx
- Attach the image to be changed and the reference image to refer to.  
- Enter the command (prompt).  
- Click the Generate button.

  **ㄴ Example keywords: change time of day, change lighting color, add fog effect, add spark effect**
