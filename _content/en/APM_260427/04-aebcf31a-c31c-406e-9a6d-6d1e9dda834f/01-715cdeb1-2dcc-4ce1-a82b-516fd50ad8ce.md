# Tutorial

**Basic instructions before generating images with the AlphaLab solution**

- 'Number of images' is the number of images to output at once. (Up to 4 images can be output.)
- All tasks are output only when you click the 'Generate' button at the end. (Shortcuts are Windows: Ctrl+Enter / Mac: Cmd+Enter.)  
- When working with a white background, it is recommended to emphasize 'white background' or 'whitebackground' in the command (prompt).  
- Images with excessively high resolution may degrade output quality. (Recommended maximum width and height of 2000 or less)
