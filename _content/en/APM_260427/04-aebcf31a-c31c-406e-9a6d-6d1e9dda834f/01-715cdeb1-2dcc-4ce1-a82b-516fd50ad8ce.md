**Basic explanation before generating images with the AlphaLab solution**

- 'Number of images' is the number of images to output at once.You can output up to 4 images.   
- For all tasks, you must click the 'Generate' button at the end to output. (Shortcuts are Windows: Ctrl+Enter, Mac: Cmd+Enter.)  
- When working with a white background, it is recommended to emphasize white background, white background, or whitebackground in the command (prompt).  
- Images with a resolution that is too high may interfere with output quality. (Recommended maximum width and height of 2000 or less)
