**Basic instructions before generating images with the AlphaLab solution**

- 'Image count' is the number of images to be output at once. You can output up to 4 images.   
- For all tasks, you must press the 'Generate' button at the end to output. (Shortcuts are Windows: Ctrl+Enter, Mac: Cmd+Enter.)  
- When working with a white background, it is recommended to emphasize it in the command (prompt) as white background, white background, or whitebackground.  
- Images with a resolution that is too high may degrade the output quality. (Recommended 3000px or less in width and height)
