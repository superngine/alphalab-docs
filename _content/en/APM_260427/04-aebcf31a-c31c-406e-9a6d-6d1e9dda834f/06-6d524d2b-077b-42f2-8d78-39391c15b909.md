# Change Background

**Edit+Fx**This is a tutorial on generating using the feature.

This is a tutorial that combines the style preservation of 'Edit' and the post-processing feature of 'FX'.

'Edit' is responsible for preserving the art style, and 'FX' is responsible for setting the mood! With just the Edit+FX button, safely add sophisticated post-processing effects without worrying about the art style changing!
