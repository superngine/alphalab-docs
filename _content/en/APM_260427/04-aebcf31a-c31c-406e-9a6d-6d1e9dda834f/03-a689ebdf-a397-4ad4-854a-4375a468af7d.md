# Character Creation

**LineBase**This is a tutorial on generating using the feature.

For rough sketch-based images, please make sure to select the 'LineBase' button!

 LineBase is a feature that generates images based on the lines of the provided image. The quality of the rough sketch is not important. 

The most important thing is the exact proportions of the image the user wants. 

When you want to generate the pose of the rough sketch with as little deformation as possible, please apply a freedom level between 10 and 13. 

The freedom level is an adjustment value for 'how much to follow this line art'. 

The lower the intensity, the stronger the influence of the rough sketch's lines (closer to the numeric value 1)

  The higher the intensity, the weaker the influence of the rough sketch's lines during generation. (closer to the numeric value 30)

  Since everyone may feel differently, try generating by freely adjusting the intensity.
