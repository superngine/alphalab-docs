# Inpaint and Liquify

## Inpaint 

**Sophisticated retouching that magically modifies only specific parts** 

A feature that allows you to newly generate or naturally change the image by selecting a specific area you want to modify with a brush or mask and writing a command (prompt). 

It drastically reduces work time and perfectly elevates the completeness of the result by allowing you to select only a specific area designated by the user and fix only the awkward parts while maintaining the overall composition of the image, without needing to regenerate it from scratch. 

## Liquify 

**Magical retouching that adds sophisticated sculptural beauty** 

Try using Liquify in the Inpaint tool for the parts of the image you want to manually correct. 

It is a partial liquify editing feature that allows you to freely correct shapes or proportions by flexibly pushing and pulling the pixels of the image within the area selected with Inpaint. 

You can finely adjust awkward body proportions or the position of facial features, and obtain clean results with a light touch just by using a prompt like 'clean up the image'.
