# Inpaint

## **Inpaint** 

**Sophisticated retouching that magically edits only specific parts** 

This feature allows you to select a specific area you want to edit using a brush or mask, and then write a command (prompt) to newly generate or naturally alter that part of the image. Without needing to regenerate from scratch, you can maintain the overall composition of the image while selecting only the specific area designated by the user to fix awkward parts, drastically reducing work time and perfectly elevating the completeness of the result.
