# Workspace

**A convenient workspace for drawing images through conversation**

The Workspace is the most basic workspace for image generation, allowing you to generate images interactively. You can check user requests and Alphalab Plus image generation results in chronological order, and conveniently use features such as regenerating previously generated images or regenerating them with references.

## LineBase

**The most perfect creation system, designed with sketches and completed with references** 

It focuses on accurately capturing the character's pose or spatial composition contained in the sketch. Show the AI directly a blueprint containing poses or spatial compositions that are difficult to express with text commands alone. By combining rough sketches and reference images, you can obtain high-quality images that well reflect the user's intentions with just very simple commands. It is not bound by the line quality or detailed drawing style of the sketch. The AI will transform the reference image and command (prompt) into exquisite details solely on the layout you designed.

## Edit+Fx

**Intelligent editing that preserves the identity of the drawing style while overcoming the limits of direction** 

Edit+Fx is an intelligent solution that firmly fixes the unique drawing style of the original image while realizing the subtle directorial variations that only existed in the creator's mind with a single prompt. 'Maintain Style', which exquisitely refines the details of the drawing depending on the situation, and 'FX', which newly synthesizes spatial images and character images without awkwardness. Get further upgraded results with these two modes!
