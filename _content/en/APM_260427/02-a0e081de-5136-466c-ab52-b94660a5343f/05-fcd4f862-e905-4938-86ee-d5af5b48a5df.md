# Workspace

**A convenient workspace for drawing images through conversation**

The Workspace is the most basic workspace for image generation, allowing you to generate images interactively. You can check user requests and Alphalab Plus image generation results in chronological order, and conveniently use features such as regenerating previously generated images and regenerating them as references.

## LineBase

**The most perfect creation system, designed with sketches and completed with references** 

It focuses on accurately capturing the character's pose or spatial composition contained in the sketch. Show the AI directly a blueprint containing a pose or spatial composition that is difficult to express with text commands alone. By combining a rough sketch and a reference image, you can obtain a high-quality image that well reflects the user's intentions with just a very simple command. It is not bound by the line quality or detailed drawing style of the sketch. The AI will change the reference image and command (prompt) into exquisite details solely on the layout you designed.

## Edit+Fx

**Intelligent editing that preserves the identity of the drawing style and overcomes the limits of directing** 

Edit+Fx is an intelligent solution that firmly fixes the unique art style of the original image while realizing the subtle directorial variations that only existed in the creator's mind with a single prompt. Depending on the situation, 'Maintain Art Style' exquisitely refines the details of the drawing, and 'FX' newly synthesizes the spatial image and character image without awkwardness. Get even more upgraded results with these two modes!
