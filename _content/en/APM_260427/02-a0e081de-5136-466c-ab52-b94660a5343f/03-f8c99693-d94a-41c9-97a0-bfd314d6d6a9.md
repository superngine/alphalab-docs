# Generated Images

Generated Images, a space where your creativity is recorded. It is a digital gallery where you can check all the images generated through the AlphaLab solution, along with detailed information such as the generation method, date, and prompt of the image at a glance. By reviewing and managing past work recipes, it maintains work consistency and maximizes image generation efficiency.
