# Generated Images

Generated Images, a space where your creativity is recorded. It is a digital gallery where you can check detailed information at a glance, such as all images generated through the Alphalab solution, generation methods, dates, and prompts for the corresponding images. 

By reviewing and managing past work recipes, it maintains work consistency and maximizes image generation efficiency.
