# 스케치 사용법

The most perfect creation system, designed with sketches and completed with references. This feature focuses on accurately capturing the character's pose or spatial composition contained in the sketch. Show the AI a blueprint containing poses or spatial compositions that are difficult to express with text commands alone. By combining a rough sketch and a reference image, you can obtain a high-quality image that accurately reflects the user's intentions with very simple commands. It is not restricted by the line quality or detailed drawing style of the sketch. The AI will transform the reference image and command (prompt) into exquisite details based solely on the layout you designed.
