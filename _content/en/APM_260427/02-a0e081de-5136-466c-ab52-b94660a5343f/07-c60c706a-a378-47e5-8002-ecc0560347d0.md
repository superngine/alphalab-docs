# LineBase

**The most perfect creation system, designed with sketches and completed with references.** 

This feature focuses on accurately capturing the character's pose or spatial composition contained in the sketch. 

Directly show the AI a blueprint containing a pose or spatial composition that is difficult to express with text commands alone.

 By combining a rough sketch and a reference image, you can obtain a high-quality image that well reflects the user's intention with just a very simple command. 

It is not bound by the line quality or detailed drawing style of the sketch.

The AI will render the reference image and command (prompt) with exquisite details exclusively on the layout you designed.
