**Edit+Fx**  
**The finishing touch of direction that breathes life into the creation, going beyond simple generation.**

The Edit+Fx button is used when you want to maintain the art style and use post-processing features.

  
It is the final stage of completing the direction, going beyond simple image coloring.  
Here are the post-processing areas covered by the AlphaLab solution.  
Directs the overall atmosphere by applying light (light source), shadows, and colors.  
Adjusts the overall tone according to the weather, time of day (day/night/sunset), and emotional line.  
The process of increasing immersion by adjusting brightness or darkness.  
Adds supernatural effects such as magic effects, flames, dust, and blowing wind.  
A feature that seamlessly composites an image of a specific space with a character image.
