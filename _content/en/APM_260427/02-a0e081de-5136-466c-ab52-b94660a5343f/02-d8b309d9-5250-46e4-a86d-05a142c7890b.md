# Thread

**Thread Management: Turning past records into value beyond just records**

Threads are spaces where all conversations and work history conducted in AlphaLab are safely recorded.  
Beyond simple conversation logs, it helps you easily find past data and utilize it for new value. Created threads can be systematically managed by categorizing them into folders, and frequently accessed important threads can be pinned to the top for quick access. Try managing your work history intuitively and efficiently through the thread window.
