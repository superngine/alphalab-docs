# Screen Layout

Alphalab Plus adopts a familiar and intuitive conversational UI, like ChatGPT, and is designed so that even first-time users can use it very easily without any complex processes.   

Not only is it easy to use, but it also provides an amazing feature that precisely reproduces the artist's unique drawing style, capturing their special artistic touch. Through this, users can quickly and flawlessly generate professional-level, high-quality images of scenes they have only imagined.   

Easily and freely unfold your own original artistic world with Alphalab Plus.
