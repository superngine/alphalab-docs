# image to image Ref

**A smart transformation that maintains the original composition and applies the style of the reference image**

This feature transforms the image by bringing in the details of the reference image while preserving the overall composition and shape of the original image. It is most efficient when you want to maintain the specific pose or layout of the original, which is difficult to describe in text, and only add the art style or design elements of the reference image.
