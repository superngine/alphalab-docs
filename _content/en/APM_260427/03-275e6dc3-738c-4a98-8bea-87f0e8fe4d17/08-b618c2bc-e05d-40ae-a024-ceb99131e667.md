# Edit\+Fx

**Intelligent editing that preserves the identity of the drawing style and overcomes the limits of direction** 

Firmly fixing the original's unique drawing style while realizing the subtle directional changes that only existed in the creator's mind, Edit+Fx is an intelligent tool that captures both creative efficiency and quality. Experience an overwhelming editing environment that perfectly implements the concept in your mind into a single scene without complex revision processes, freely switching between 'Edit' to enhance the completeness of the drawing and 'Fx' direction to harmoniously connect characters and backgrounds!
