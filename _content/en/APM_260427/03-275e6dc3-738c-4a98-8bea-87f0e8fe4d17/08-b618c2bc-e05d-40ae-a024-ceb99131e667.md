# Edit\+Fx

{{manual-highlight:#dcfce7}}**This section is currently scheduled for an update.**{{/manual-highlight}}

**Intelligent editing that preserves the identity of the drawing style and overcomes the limits of directing** 

Edit+Fx is an intelligent solution that firmly fixes the unique drawing style of the original image while realizing subtle directorial variations that only existed in the creator's mind with a single prompt.

'Maintain Style' for elaborately refining the details of the drawing depending on the situation, and 'FX' for newly synthesizing spatial and character images without awkwardness.  
Get further upgraded results with these two modes!
