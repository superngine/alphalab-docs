# Rough Sketch

**The most perfect creation system, designed with sketches and completed with references** 

This feature focuses on accurately capturing the character's pose or spatial composition contained in the sketch.

Show the AI directly a blueprint containing poses or spatial compositions that are difficult to express with text commands alone! By combining a rough sketch and reference images, you can obtain high-quality images that well reflect the user's intentions with just very simple commands. 

It is not bound by the line quality or detailed drawing style of the sketch.

The AI will transform the reference images and commands (prompts) into elaborate details solely on top of the layout you designed.
