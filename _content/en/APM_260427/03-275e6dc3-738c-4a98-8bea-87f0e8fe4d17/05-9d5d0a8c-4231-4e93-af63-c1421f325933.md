# Rough Sketch

**The most perfect creation system, designed with sketches and completed with references** 

This feature focuses on accurately capturing the pose of the character or the spatial composition contained in the sketch. Show the AI directly a blueprint containing a pose or spatial composition that is difficult to express with text commands alone! By combining a rough sketch and reference images, you can obtain high-quality images that well reflect the user's intentions with very simple commands. It is not bound by the line quality or detailed drawing style of the sketch. The AI will simply transform the reference images and commands (prompts) into exquisite details on the layout you designed.
