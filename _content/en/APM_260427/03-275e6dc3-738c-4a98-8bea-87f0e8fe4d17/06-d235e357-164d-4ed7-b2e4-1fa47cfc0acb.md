# Inpaint

**Precise retouching that magically modifies only specific parts**

This is a feature that allows you to newly generate or naturally change the image by selecting a specific area you want to modify with a brush or mask and entering a command (prompt). 

Without needing to regenerate from scratch, it maintains the overall composition of the image while allowing you to select only a specific area designated by the user to fix awkward parts, drastically reducing work time and perfectly elevating the completeness of the result.
