# Image to Image

**The moment your image is reborn as a more special artwork by applying a new style** 

Based on the overall composition of the original image, the shapes of objects and figures, and the poses of the characters, the art style, mood, and fine details of the image are transformed according to the command (prompt). 

Simply put, it is a feature that upgrades an existing image to the desired feel. 

The biggest difference from T2I is whether the AI generates an image from a blank slate or takes an existing image and applies changes over it. 

To generate the image you want, show a single image to the AI!
