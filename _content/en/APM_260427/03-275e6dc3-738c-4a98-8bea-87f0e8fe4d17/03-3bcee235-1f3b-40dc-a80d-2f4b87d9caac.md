# Image to Image

**The moment your image is reborn as a more special artwork by applying a new style** 

Based on the overall composition, the shape of objects and figures, and the pose of the figures in the original image, the art style, mood, and fine details of the image are transformed according to the command (prompt). 

Simply put, it is a feature that upgrades an existing image to the desired feel. 

The biggest difference from T2I is whether the AI generates an image from a blank slate or takes an existing image and adds changes on top of it. 

To generate the image you want, please show a single image to the AI.
