# Inpaint Liquify

**Magical retouching that adds sophisticated sculptural beauty** 

Try using Liquify in the Inpaint tool for the parts of the image you want to manually correct! It is a partial liquify editing feature that allows you to freely correct shapes or proportions by flexibly pushing and pulling the pixels of the image within the area selected with Inpaint. You can finely adjust awkward body proportions or the position of facial features, and achieve clean results with a light touch and just a prompt to clean up the target that needs modification.
