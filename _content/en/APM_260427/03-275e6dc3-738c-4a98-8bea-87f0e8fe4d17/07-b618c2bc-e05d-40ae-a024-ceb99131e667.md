# Edit\+Fx

**Intelligent editing that preserves the drawing's identity and overcomes the limits of directing** 

Edit+Fx is an intelligent tool that captures both creative efficiency and quality by firmly fixing the original's unique art style while realizing the subtle directing changes that only existed in the creator's mind. 

An overwhelming editing environment that realizes the concept in your head into a perfect scene without complex revision processes, freely switching between 'Edit' which enhances the perfection of the drawing and 'Fx' directing which harmoniously connects the character and the background 

Experience it!
