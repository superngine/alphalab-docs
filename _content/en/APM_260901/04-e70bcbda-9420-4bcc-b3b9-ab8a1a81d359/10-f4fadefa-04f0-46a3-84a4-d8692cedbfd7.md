# Change field of view or angle \(T\+Ref\)

## **The most flexible image transformation system, directed by text and specified by reference (T+Ref)**

- This feature uses text prompts as the main input, but combines them with reference images to focus on perfectly transforming the image in the direction the user wants. 
- Show the AI directly with a reference image the details and mood that are difficult to explain with text alone.
- By combining clear text commands and reference images, you can obtain new results that go beyond the limits of the original image with very simple operations.   
  \- In particular, this feature **dynamically changing the angle of the subject**or, **newly expanding and adjusting the field of view and proportions of the image**is most effective when you need to do so.   
  \- There is no need to be bound by the composition of the original image. Based solely on the commands you enter as text, the AI takes the reference image and   
   recreates it into a high-quality image with a completely new angle and perfect proportions.
