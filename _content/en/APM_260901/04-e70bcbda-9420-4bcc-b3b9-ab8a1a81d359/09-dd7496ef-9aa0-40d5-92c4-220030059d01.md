# Add Directing Fx

## **Edit+Fx  A mode that can be used for post-processing to increase the density of a scene**

- A feature that maximizes the density and liveliness of a scene by overlaying weather, time of day (day/night/sunset, etc.), and supernatural energy onto an ordinary cut. 
- It elaborately implements various visual effects that determine the directing quality of a webtoon, such as weather, time of day, and supernatural energy, using only text. 
- The AI analyzes the light source and spatial information of the image on its own, ensuring that special effects blend naturally into the screen without looking out of place.  
- You can create a three-dimensional sense of space without complex layer work using only text commands. 
  - It adjusts the temperature of the scene by instantly changing a classroom landscape with a clear sky into an urgent scene with a rainstorm, or by switching to a blue tone where the cold dawn air can be felt. 
  - It generates dynamic effects such as exploding flames and swirling auras (Aura) according to the character's emotions. 
  - Dramatic directing is possible by adding scattering petals, dust particles, and lens flare effects across the entire screen.

**&lt; Tips &gt;**  

- **Setting detailed descriptions in the command (prompt)**  
**:** First, try setting the overall color (tone) according to whether the cut is day or night, or what emotion the character is feeling, using a command (prompt). 
- **Utilizing the Inpaint feature**  
**:** If the effect is too strong and covers the character, try using the Inpaint feature together. By designating the area where the effect will be applied in advance, a much more restrained and refined direction is possible.
