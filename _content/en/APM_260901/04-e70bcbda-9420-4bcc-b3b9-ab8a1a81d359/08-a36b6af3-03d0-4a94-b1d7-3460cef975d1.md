# Natural Edit \(Edit\+Fx\)

## **Edit+Fx (A mode that supports naturally editing specific elements without redrawing**)

This feature is used when you want to naturally change specific elements while keeping the overall art style of the image and the shape of the character intact.   
It prioritizes preserving the original line feel, coloring style, and character's impression, reconstructing only specific objects according to the entered command (prompt). 

- When the character's face or pose is perfect, but you only want to change the design or pattern of the outfit 
- When you want to maintain the background composition but only change the types of placed props 
- When you want to modify the texture or color of a specific part within the artist's unique style

**&lt; Tips &gt;**

- Informing the parts that must never be changed (texture, features, area) first is the key to maintaining the art style.
- When changing outfits, try specifying the texture, such as "smooth silk material", "rough leather texture", or "soft cotton material".   
Even if you are only changing the outfit, please mention the character's physical features at the beginning of the prompt, such as "keeping the sharp eyes" or "leaving the round eyes and freckles as they are"!
- When changing only the character's outfit or the prop in their hand, try using the Inpaint feature to specify the area before entering the prompt.
