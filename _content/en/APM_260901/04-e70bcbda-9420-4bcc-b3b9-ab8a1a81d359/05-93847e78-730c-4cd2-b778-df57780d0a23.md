# Image Generation with Just a Prompt

## **The moment your imagination comes to life as a piece of art with just a prompt** 

- Even without complex drawing skills, you can freely control the desired mood, subject, lighting, and art style using only language, making it optimized for instantly visualizing creative ideas. 
- This is a feature that generates a new image in the trained engine's art style when the user enters a sufficient text description of the image they want to generate into the prompt window.

**&lt; Tips &gt;**

- **Detailed description:** Rather than entering "woman", try something like "a woman with long brown hair wearing a yellow short-sleeved dress, sitting and smiling on a sofa with morning sunlight streaming in"   
                          The more detailed you describe the time, place, and condition, the higher the quality of the generated image. 
- **Utilize style keywords:** Try adding keywords for your desired art style, such as photorealistic, anime style, or fairytale-like.
