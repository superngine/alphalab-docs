# Generate with Rough and Reference Images

## **The most perfect creation system, designed with sketches and completed with references** 

- This feature focuses on accurately capturing the character's pose or spatial composition contained in the sketch. 
- Directly show the AI a blueprint containing poses or spatial compositions that are difficult to express with text commands alone! 
- By combining a rough sketch and a reference image, you can obtain a high-quality image that well reflects the user's intentions with just a very simple command. 
- It is not restricted by the line quality or detailed drawing style of the sketch. The AI will transform the reference image and command (prompt) into exquisite details based solely on the layout you designed.
