# Reflecting Pose and Composition Pose

## **Pose (A mode that supports generating images that match sketches or storyboards**)

Creating an artwork often involves dramatic compositions and poses tailored to the situation being depicted, rather than cliché expressions.  
It accurately captures the character's pose or spatial composition in the sketch or storyboard and supports generating precise images using reference images.

- Directly show the AI a blueprint containing poses or spatial compositions that are difficult to express with text prompts alone. 
- It is not restricted by the line quality or detailed art style of the sketch.
- However, if the character's body type depicted in the sketch differs too much from the character sheet, it may cause confusion.
