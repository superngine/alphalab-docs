# Generated Image List

# **Records both created images and recipes, not just a simple generation history** 

- A digital gallery where you can view detailed information at a glance, such as all images generated through Alphalab, generation methods, dates, and prompts for the corresponding images. 
- It contributes to maintaining work consistency and improving image generation efficiency by reviewing and managing past work recipes.
- A button is provided to add the generated image directly to the prompt window so it can be reused after changing the pose or outfit.
