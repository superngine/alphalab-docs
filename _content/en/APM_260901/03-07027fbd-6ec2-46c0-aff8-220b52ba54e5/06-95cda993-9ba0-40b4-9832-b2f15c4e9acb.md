## 4) Pose Mode

**Supports accurately capturing the poses of characters or spatial compositions contained in sketches or storyboards** 

Focuses on accurately capturing the poses of characters or spatial compositions contained in sketches. Show the AI directly a blueprint containing poses or spatial compositions that are difficult to express with text commands alone. By combining rough sketches and reference images, you can obtain high-quality images that well reflect the user's intentions with just very simple commands. It is not constrained by the line quality or detailed drawing style of the sketch.

## 5) Edit+Fx Mode

**Intelligent editing support that preserves the identity of the drawing style while overcoming the limits of direction**

Edit+Fx is an intelligent solution that firmly fixes the unique drawing style of the original image while realizing the subtle directorial variations that only existed in the creator's mind with a single prompt. 'Maintain Style', which elaborately refines the details of the drawing depending on the situation, and 'FX', which newly synthesizes spatial images and character images without awkwardness. Get even more upgraded results with these two modes!

## 6) T+Ref Mode

**Supports the most flexible transformation, instructed by text and materialized by reference**

T+Ref mode focuses on perfectly transforming the reference image into the image the user wants, while using the text prompt as the main input. It is most effective when presenting details and atmospheres that are difficult to explain with text alone as reference images, and changing the composition, angle of view, sense of distance, etc., with clear text commands.
