# How to Become Familiar with AI



1. **Following the tutorials**  
 - The tutorials in the manual display prompts and reference images.   
  \- By following the instructions sequentially to generate images, you can naturally learn the features and characteristics.  

2. **Trying to generate images in new ways**   
  \- Unlike general-purpose AIs, AlphaLab has virtually no usage limits, so you can get familiar with it by freely generating various images as much as you want.   
  \- Rather than generating images only through familiar methods, continuously researching and trying new methods and procedures will help you understand AI more deeply.   

3. **Getting help from experts**  
  \- If the desired image is not generated well or you do not fully understand a feature, please contact the person in charge via email or messenger, and   
  developers or experienced internal artists will quickly help you resolve it.   
  \- When inquiring, so that we can also check the system, providing *++the time of generation, the intended use of the image, and the methods you tried++* together will greatly help us respond quickly.  

4. **Sharing know-how**  
  \- Regarding what is more efficient in terms of which image to generate first, which image to select, and what modifications to make during the image generation, selection, and modification process using AlphaLab,   
  it will vary depending on the characteristics of the artwork or art style, the target quality level and schedule, and the type of image you are trying to create.   
  \- By acquiring know-how from other artists and becoming able to respond efficiently to various situations, you will be able to utilize AlphaLab stably and efficiently.   
  \- We will continuously run training sessions and seminars where you can share know-how with other artists.
