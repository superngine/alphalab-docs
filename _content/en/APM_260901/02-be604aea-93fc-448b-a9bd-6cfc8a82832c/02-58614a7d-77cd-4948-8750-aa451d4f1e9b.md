# Basic Characteristics of Generative AI that Creators Should Know  


1. **Need for Detailed Instructions**   
With the advancement of AI, it can now infer a significant amount of uninstructed content, but there is no guarantee that this inference will match the creator's intention.   
  \- Therefore, it is advantageous to provide instructions as detailed as possible or to use specialized AI tools that provide default instructions set by experts for each project.  

2. **Need to Actively Use Reference Images**   
A lot of information that is difficult to explain with text, such as composition, form, and color, can be easily conveyed through multiple reference images. উভ  
Reference images serve to restrict the visual decision-making space of the generative model itself and condition it in the desired direction.  

3. **Effort Required to Maintain Consistency**   
Even with the advancement of AI, if a new image is generated independently, it is still normal for it to be generated differently every time due to the nature of AI. উভ  
However, overall consistency can be improved by creating a separate dedicated model or utilizing LoRA, etc., and   
  simply by utilizing the same reference image or using previous generation results as conditions in editing, a significant level of visual consistency can be maintained.  

4. **Prompts are not commands, but 'directional signals'**   
Words at the beginning of a prompt often have a greater impact than those at the end, so visually important parts should be placed at the front. উভ  
Abstract words are reflected weaker or stronger than expected, and spatial/quantitative/attribute connections are weak. উভ  
Therefore, rather than generating a complex image all at once, it is often more efficient to generate images centered on core objects and then composite them.  

5. **Need for a Step-by-Step Approach উভ**  
AI is surprisingly human-like, but it does not understand or intend the world in the same way humans do. উভ  
Therefore, errors are highly likely to occur in rare situations, new combinations, or physically complex conditions. উভ  
Creators who need a new expression style should  generate by adding objects step-by-step or generate by elements and then composite them.
