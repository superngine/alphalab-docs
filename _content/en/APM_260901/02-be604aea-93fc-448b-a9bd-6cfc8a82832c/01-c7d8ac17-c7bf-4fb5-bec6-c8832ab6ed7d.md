## When using the tools, why is **learning about AI** necessary?

- Although the usage of AI generative tools itself is simpler compared to general drawing or editing tools,   
most of the image generation and modification, rather than explicit programming, **is processed by AI, so identical results are not guaranteed even if the same values are input.**
- In particular, AI does not explain the generation process or why it succeeded or failed, making immediate and accurate problem-solving fundamentally impossible.  
Therefore, without learning the characteristics of AI and the know-how of working with it, it is difficult to move beyond beginner-level results.
- However, just as you would get to know a colleague, if you learn a few important characteristics and workflow know-how, you can easily make good use of a great colleague named AI.
