# Purpose and Scope of Use for AlphaLab AI

## Development Purpose and Use

- AlphaLab AI is an AI solution for professional creators producing commercial content.
- Its goal is not simply to create plausible images quickly, but to support systematic production to enable consistent long-term serialization.
- We plan to expand the scope of support in the future to include content bible generation through original work analysis, as well as short-form animation and chatbot production.
