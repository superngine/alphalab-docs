# Character Pose Generation

**Pose** feature tutorial on how to generate images.

- Try generating the desired character pose using a pose sketch or a 3D pose dummy.
- Images are generated using a base image for the pose and a reference image containing the character's information.
- The most important thing is the exact proportion of the base image containing the pose the user wants. 
- When you want to generate the pose from the pose sketch with as little deformation as possible, please set the degree of freedom between L and M. 
  - The degree of freedom determines the range of change. 
  - The lower the intensity, the stronger the influence of the pose sketch's lines (L),
  - and the higher the intensity, the weaker the influence of the pose sketch's lines during generation. (H)
- Since everyone may feel differently, try generating by freely adjusting the intensity. 
  - The default values for the degree of freedom and density are 'M'.

**Pose** feature, try generating images using the **Sketch** mode.

- The Sketch mode in the Pose feature can generate images by reflecting more precise rough sketches or line art.
- Use it by activating the Sketch mode to ON.
- This feature is used when you want to reflect detailed expressions such as facial expressions or hair movement depicted in a rough sketch.
- When you want to generate the pose from the rough sketch with as little deformation as possible, please set the degree of freedom between L and M. 
  - The degree of freedom determines the range of change. 
  - The lower the intensity, the stronger the influence of the rough sketch's lines (L),
  - and the higher the intensity, the weaker the influence of the rough sketch's lines during generation. (H)
- Since everyone may feel differently, try generating by freely adjusting the intensity. 
  - The default values for the degree of freedom and density are 'M'.
