# Post\-processing

**Edit+Fx**This is a tutorial on generating using the feature.

- Use the 'FX' feature in Edit+FX. 
- Use this when you want to precisely implement dramatic effects for webtoons, such as weather, time of day, and supernatural energy, using only text commands without cumbersome manual work.
- The AI analyzes the image's light source (direction of light) and spatial information on its own, allowing special effects to blend in naturally as if they were in the original drawing, rather than looking out of place.
